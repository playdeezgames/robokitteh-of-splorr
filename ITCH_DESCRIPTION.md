**Robokitteh of SPLORR!!**

*A metaphor about herding cats. In SPLORR!!*

You are a robot. The room is blue. There are cats in it, and a door in the wall.

Your job is to get every cat out through the door. The cats do not know this is your job, and they would not care if they did.

Your battery is draining the whole time.

**How to play**

- **Arrow keys or WASD** to move. That's the only input.
- The game is **turn based**. Nothing happens until you move.
- Every move costs battery. At 0% you are a very expensive paperweight.
- **Bump into a solar terminal** to recharge. It refills slowly, so you can drain it.
- **Push barrels.** They cost extra battery to shove, but cats can't walk through them.
- Cats wander at random, and some of them just sit down.
- Walking into a wall is allowed. It costs battery. You will do it anyway.
- When a cat finds the door, it leaves, and the door moves. Get them all out to clear the level. The next level has more cats.
